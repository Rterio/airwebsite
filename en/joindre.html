Our training environment

What you'll find with us, and what we're looking for

What we offer

  • A project rooted in clinical practice, working with patients and respirologists at the IUCPQ
  • Close supervision by a clinician-scientist and an experienced research team
  • Opportunities to present your results at conferences and to publish
  • Support with scholarship applications (e.g. FRQS, CIHR)
  • An internationally recognized research centre in respiratory, cardiac and metabolic health

Who we're looking for

  • Training in health sciences, biomedical sciences or a related field
  • An interest in clinical research and working with patients
  • Rigour, independence and a taste for teamwork
  • Good French skills; scientific English is an asset

How to apply

Applications are sent by email, with the documents below

The steps

  1. Prepare your documents

    Gather the documents listed here, in PDF format.

  2. Send your application

    By email, to the address below, stating the level you're applying for in the subject line.

  3. A first meeting

    If your profile fits our projects, we'll invite you to an interview, in person or by video call.

  4. Program admission

    We then support you with your Université Laval admission application and your scholarship applications.

Documents to include

Curriculum vitae
Education, research or work experience, and publications or presentations if any.
Transcripts
From all your university degrees; an unofficial copy is fine at this stage.
Cover letter
One page on your research interests and what draws you to our projects.
Two references
Name and contact information of two people who can speak to your work (professors, internship supervisors).
