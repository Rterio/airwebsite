Improving diagnosis and care pathways for people with asthma

Our work is organized around three complementary themes, from the clinic to the lab.

Severe asthma and airway inflammation

Understanding the inflammatory mechanisms that make some asthma hard to control despite optimal treatment.

Learn more

Faster care pathways

Shortening the time between first symptoms, a confirmed diagnosis and access to the right treatment.

Learn more

Inflammatory phenotyping

Characterizing each patient's inflammatory profile (induced sputum, biomarkers) to guide treatment choices.

Learn more
